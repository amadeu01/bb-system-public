bb-system

A coach and the person they coach, sharing training, diet, check-ins and progress in one tracked conversation.

Join the beta Beta · under development
BB Student

For the student

  • Today's training, diet and supplements, exactly as the coach wrote them.
  • Log workouts with sets and loads, and the meals you actually ate.
  • Send a daily check-in with photos, including what went wrong.
  • Chat with your coach in one place.
BB Coach

For the coach

  • Your assigned students and where each one stands.
  • Review check-ins and reply; new, seen and replied are always clear.
  • Protocols for diet, training and phytotherapy, published to the student's app.
  • The same chat, on your side.
Tracked

Progress stays tracked

  • Evolution over time: adherence, loads, measurements and health readings in one view.
  • Every check-in, reply and message is kept, so nothing gets lost in loose chats.
  • Offline-safe: what you type is saved on the device and sent when the connection returns.

Join the beta

bb-system is under development and in alpha/beta testing. The iOS apps, BB Student and BB Coach, are distributed through TestFlight by invitation. An Android app for students is in testing as well.

Expect rough edges: screens change between builds, and the beta backend may be reset. Testers are told by e-mail before that happens.

To get an invitation, send an e-mail with the subject "bb-system beta" and say whether you are a coach or a student.
